## Key Structural Choices That Worked

1. **No boilerplate** — Opens with "I'm Jordan — a self-taught builder" not "I am writing to express my interest"
2. **Shipped over credentials** — Three specific projects, each with a proof-of-life claim
3. **Specific company reference** — "constrained autonomy" ties directly to their Chief Scientist's published work
4. **Honest time disclosure** — Amazon Flex schedule stated upfront rather than glossed over
5. **Intern angle reframed** — "I can prove shipping velocity without a degree" turns a potential negative into a positive
6. **Closing hooks into their work** — Not "hope to hear from you" but "constrained autonomy in multi-agent payment systems"
