# Voice Capture Script

Send this to the collaborator when asking them to record.

---

Hi! Please record a short voice clip for me. Here's what to do:

1. Find a **quiet place** — no TV, no music, no background noise
2. Open your phone's **Voice Recorder** app
3. Read the script below in your **natural voice** — don't try to sound professional, just speak normally
4. Send the **audio file** back here

**Script (English version):**

> "Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I'm from [Your Location] and I'm excited to learn about AI and technology. This is a great opportunity for me and I want to help the team with my voice."

**Script (Bisaya version):**

> "Hello, ako si [Your Name]. Taga [Your Location] ko. Ganahan ko makat-on ug bag-ong mga butang bahin sa AI ug technology. Nindot kaayo ni nga oportunidad para nako. Sa umaabot, gusto ko makatabang sa team pinaagi sa akong tingog."

**Tips:**
- 30-60 seconds is perfect
- Speak clearly but naturally
- If you make a mistake, just continue — don't restart
- .mp3, .m4a, or .ogg format is fine

Thank you! 🎤
