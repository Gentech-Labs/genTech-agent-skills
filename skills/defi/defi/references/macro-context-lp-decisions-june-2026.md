# Macro Context for LP Decisions — June 2026 Case Study

## The Liquidity Crisis Pattern

**Date:** June 11, 2026
**Context:** Everything is red — gold, silver, stocks, crypto all falling together

### What's Happening

1. **Gold/Silver broke safe haven status**
   - Gold dropped from highs, silver crashed 31% in January 2026 (CME margin hike to 15%)
   - Gold has broken its 200-day moving average
   - Gold is now correlated with equities instead of being a hedge
   - The "flight to safety" trade is broken

2. **New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh is HAWKISH**
   - Powell's term ended May 15, 2026
   - Warsh's first FOMC meeting: June 16-17, 2026
   - Despite market expecting rate CUTS, Warsh signals possible rate HIKES
   - CPI and PPI elevated, jobs strong
   - Bond markets already pricing in hikes

3. **Smart money in dry powder mode**
   - Not rotating into safe havens (they're failing)
   - Not buying crypto (rates might go UP)
   - Sitting in stablecoins, fiat, cash
   - Waiting for June 16-17 FOMC clarity

4. **Everything falling together = mechanical liquidation**
   - NOT fundamental repricing
   - Margin calls force selling across ALL asset classes
   - Safe havens fail because they're sold to cover margin elsewhere
   - Smart money isn't making directional bets — they're de-risking

### Implications for LP Strategy

- **June 17 FOMC is the fork in the road**
  - Warsh signals cuts → everything rips, LP captures upside
  - Warsh signals hikes → more pain short-term, limit bids fill at better prices

- **Buy zone system still works**
  - AVAX at $6.50 = Accumulate zone ($5-$7)
  - Limit bid at $5.50-$5.80 = near Deep Value
  - Two ways to win regardless of FOMC outcome

- **DCA philosophy validated**
  - Zone DCA > Calendar DCA
  - Split entries across support levels
  - "Buy the dip on the average of the dip"
