# Thailand Cultural Intelligence

## Laws
- **Visa:** US citizens get 30 days visa-free
- **Drinking age:** 20
- **Drugs:** SEVERE penalties — death penalty possible for trafficking
- **Smoking:** Banned in public places, beaches, temples
- **Dress code:** MUST cover shoulders and knees at temples (strictly enforced)
- **Monarchy:** CRITICAL — never disrespect the King or Royal Family (15 years prison)
- **Photography:** No photos of military, government buildings, or royal property
- **Tipping:** Not expected but appreciated (20-50 THB)
- **Emergency:** 191 (police), 199 (tourist police), 1669 (ambulance)

## Cultural Norms
- "Wai" — slight bow with hands together (return when offered)
- Never touch anyone's head (most sacred)
- Don't point feet at people or sacred objects
- Remove shoes before entering homes, temples, some shops
- Dress modestly at temples, behave quietly
- NEVER criticize the Royal Family (lèse-majesté laws)
- Buddha images: treat with respect, don't use as decoration

## Safety
- Use Grab or BTS/MRT in Bangkok
- Negotiate tuk-tuk price first
- Some southern provinces need caution (Yala, Pattani, Narathiwat)
- Monsoon season May-October

## Food/Water
- Tap water NOT safe
- Street food generally safe, eat where locals eat
- Must-try: Pad Thai, green curry, mango sticky rice, som tam
- Ask for "mai phet" (not spicy) if needed

## Songkran (Thai New Year)
- April 13-15 annually
- Water festival — everyone gets wet
- Sacred rituals: pour water on Buddha images, on elders' hands
- Public water fights in streets
- Travel tips: waterproof bag for phone, quick-dry clothes
