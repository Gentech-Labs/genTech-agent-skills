# Switzerland / Schengen Visa from the Philippines (Aug 2026)

Researched from VFS Global Philippines tourism checklist + Swiss FDFA (eda.admin.ch).

## Key fact
Philippine passport holders need a **Schengen visa Type C** for Switzerland tourism (up to 90 days within any 180-day period). No visa-free entry.

## Documents (tourism checklist)
- Application form — free download from SEM website; black/blue ink, block letters; minors need both parents' signatures
- Passport — valid **6+ months after** planned stay, with **2 consecutive blank pages**; copies of all relevant pages + copies of past Schengen/UK/US/Canada visas (10 years)
- 2 passport photos — color, light background, <6 months old, full face visible (no head covers except religious)
- Roundtrip flight **reservation** (do NOT purchase ticket yet — embassy not responsible for losses)
- Travel insurance — minimum **€30,000** coverage for the entire Schengen area, entire stay; no sub-limits/exclusions
- Accommodation proof for EVERY night of the trip (hotel reservations, rental contract)
- Detailed daily itinerary
- Financial means — stamped/signed original bank certificate + bank statements showing 6 months activity (+ Transfer Certificate of Title if applicable)
- If employed: original company letter on letterhead (position, hire date, salary, approved leave dates, confirmation of continued employment) + income tax return
- If self-employed: DTI/SEC registration, income tax return, business financial statement
- If married: marriage certificate (+ birth certificates of children)
- Minors traveling without both parents: notarized consent letter(s) + DSWD clearance + copies of parents' ID

## Fees (VFS Philippines)
- Adult (12+): **€90 (~₱6,300)**
- Child 6–11: €45 (~₱3,150)
- Child 0–5: free
- Non-refundable even if refused

## Process
- Apply at **VFS Global, 6th Floor Circuit Corporate Center 2, Circuit Makati** — by appointment only
- Application max **180 days before departure**; processing takes weeks-months — apply early
- Biometrics/fingerprints mandatory → personal appearance required
- Submit documents unstaple; incomplete docs → likely refusal
- Embassy of Switzerland (Manila): 24F BDO Equitable Tower, 8751 Paseo de Roxas, Makati; visa section +63 2 8845 4545 (press 1)
- Applicants must reside in the Philippines (non-residents apply at their country of residence)
