# Cebu & Lapu-Lapu City — First Timer's Guide

## Geography (Critical — First-Timers Get This Wrong)

- **Cebu Island** — The big island. Cebu City is here.
- **Mactan Island** — A smaller island right next to Cebu Island, connected by two bridges (~10 min drive).
- **Lapu-Lapu City** — The city that covers the entire Mactan Island. It is NOT a remote island — it's a proper city with malls, hospitals, 7-Elevens, Grab cars, and the airport.
- **Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB)** — Located on Mactan Island, in Lapu-Lapu City. 10-15 min from most resorts.

**Analogy:** Lapu-Lapu is like Covington/Newport across the river from Cincinnati — separate city, connected by bridges, part of the same metro area.

## Getting There

| Route | Time | Cost | Notes |
|-------|------|------|-------|
| CVG → CEB (one-way) | ~18-24 hrs (1 stop) | $429-550 | Sep is cheapest month. Airlines: Alaska (SEA), American/Cathay (LAX/HKG) |
| MNL → CEB (one-way) | 1 hr 20 min | $23-35 | Cebu Pacific, PAL, AirAsia — 80+ daily |
| CEB → MNL (one-way) | 1 hr 20 min | $23-30 | Same airlines, same price range |

**Cebu to Manila — NO bus option.** Cebu is an island. Options:
- ✈️ Flight: $23-30, 80 min ✅ Best
- 🚢 Ferry (2GO Travel): $24-47, 22-25 hrs ❌ Not worth it

## Where to Stay in Lapu-Lapu

Budget range: **$20-50/night** (3-star and below). September is off-peak (wet season) = lowest prices.

**Book on Agoda** — best prices for SE Asia. Expedia for US-origin long-haul flights.

## What to Do

- **Lapu-Lapu Monument & Shrine** — Site of the Battle of Mactan (Magellan's defeat)
- **Nalusuan Island Marine Sanctuary** — Snorkeling, starfish, sandbar
- **Island hopping** — Olango Island, Hilutungan, Caohagan
- **Diving** — Mactan has good coral gardens, easy dives
- **Mactan Newtown** — Modern dining, bars, nightlife strip
- **Cebu City day trip** — 30 min across the bridge for temples, lechon, Sinulog

## First Timer Tips

1. **September = wet season** — Afternoon showers that pass fast. Mornings clear. Pack a light rain jacket.
2. **Grab is your friend** — Works great in Cebu. Avoid random taxis.
3. **Tap water NOT safe** — Bottled water everywhere.
4. **Must eat** — Cebu lechon (best in PH), sutukil (grilled seafood), danggit (dried fish breakfast)
5. **Resort day passes** — Many resorts sell day passes for pools/beach even if you're not staying
6. **Cash is still king** outside big resorts — bring enough pesos
7. **"Po" and "Opo"** — Add when talking to elders
8. **Don't touch anyone's head** — considered sacred
9. **Weather** — 28-32°C, humid, short tropical downpours then sun
