# Southeast Asia Booking Platform Comparison

## Agoda
**Best for:** Southeast Asia specifically
- HQ in Bangkok, stronger SE Asian relationships
- 15-20% commission (lower than Booking.com)
- Agoda Homes: private homes without Airbnb's 16% guest service fee
- Members-only Secret Deals
- AgodaCash rewards
- Pay at hotel often available
- Prices 10-20% cheaper than Booking.com in SE Asia

## Booking.com
**Best for:** Global coverage, flexible cancellation
- 15-25% commission (higher than Agoda)
- Genius loyalty discounts (Level 1/2/3)
- Most flexible cancellation policies
- Larger review database
- Usually pay at property
- Prices 10-20% higher than Agoda in SE Asia

## Airbnb
**Best for:** Unique stays, longer trips, groups
- 14-16% guest service fee added at checkout
- Cleaning fee can add $20-50 per stay
- Monthly discounts 20-30% (best for 28+ day stays)
- More unique/quirky listings
- Stronger community/review system
- Rules change frequently — check local regulations

## Price Example (10 nights, Makati)
| Platform | Nightly | Fees | Total |
|----------|---------|------|-------|
| Agoda Homes | $45 | $0 | $450 |
| Booking.com | $52 | $0 | $520 |
| Airbnb | $45 | $68 service + $40 cleaning | $558 |

## Recommendation Logic
- **Short stay (1-3 nights):** Agoda or Booking.com (no fees)
- **Week+ vacation:** Agoda (best SE Asia prices)
- **Monthly stay:** Airbnb (20-30% discount)
- **Groups:** Airbnb (multiple bedrooms)
- **Budget backpacker:** Hostelworld
- **Unique experience:** Airbnb (treehouses, boats)
- **Business travel:** Booking.com (flexible, rewards)

## Airbnb Rules Changing
- Global service fee now 14-16% (was 12-14%)
- Some cities limiting cleaning fees
- Philippines: Many Makati condos prohibit <30 day rentals
- BGC: Some buildings ban Airbnb entirely
- Thailand: Minimum 30-day stays in many condos
- Bali: New restrictions in some villages
