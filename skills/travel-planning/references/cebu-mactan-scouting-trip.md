# Cebu / Mactan Scouting Trip — Research Bank (Aug 22, 2026)

Session-specific researched prices for Jordan's Sep 8–15, 2026 Cebu/Mactan trip. Reusable
for the return trip and for future SE-Asia scouting itineraries.

## The framing: "scouting trip," not a splurge
Jordan's own words: they're NOT saving on the first trip (they've had emergencies), NO tours,
just **scoping out how cheap things are**. Priority: groceries, cheap getting-around, nice
restaurants to filter, parks, city overlook with cost, malls, night markets, nightlife, and
what's near the stay. Plan for a RETURN trip. This changes the whole recommendation logic:
lead with cheap-living data, not day tours.

## Trip facts
- Dates: Sep 8–15, 2026 · 7 nights · 2 people (Jordan + Celine)
- Stay: Seukyang Village 1 Phase 3, Marigondon Beach Rd, Lapu-Lapu (Mactan Island)
- Budget: $300 for 2, ~₱56/$1 → ~$21/person/day. Accommodation already covered.
- Only 2 trips out into Cebu City (inner city); rest chill on Mactan.

## Getting around (verified cheap)
- Grab Mactan→Cebu City: ~₱300-400 (~$5-7). Within Mactan: ₱100-200 (~$2-4).
- Jeepney: ₱13-20 (~$0.25-0.35)/ride — the true local option.
- Trike: ₱30-50 short hops — negotiate before riding.

## City overlook (the "look down on the city" spot)
- **Tops Lookout (The Circle at TOPS)** — 360° Cebu City view, BEST at sunset/night (open 24hrs).
  - Entrance ₱100/person (~$1.80); rooftop +₱50; senior/PWD ₱70.
  - Cheapest: TOPS Shuttle from IT Park terminal (Lahug) — ₱200 roundtrip INCLUDING entrance.
  - Nearby: Temple of Leah (₱100), Sirao Flower Garden (₱100-200), La Vie Parisienne cafe.
  - ~$25-30 for 2 all-in with Grab + entrance.

## Night markets (like Thailand)
- **Colon Night Market** (Colon St, Cebu City) — Fri/Sat/Sun nights, street-food + flea market.
  ₱200-400 to eat for 2. Caveat: Carbon Night Market reports say closed; Colon is the reliable one.

## Nightlife — Mactan Newtown (the walkable strip)
- LUX Lounge & Bar, Ibiza Beach Club, Kangaroo Bar & Bistro, Azure, Liv Superclub, Room 801.
- Mactan Newtown Beach **night pass ₱850 (~$15)** incl ₱600 food credit — sunset pool + dinner.

## Parks & green space near Seukyang Village 1
- Mactan Shrine — free, 20m Lapu-Lapu statue, landscaped gardens (15-20 min away).
- Mactan Island Memorial Garden (~1km) · HydroFarm Lettuce Garden (~0.8km) · Tonggo Beach (18-min walk).
- Mactan Newtown — 30-hectare walkable township with beach + gardens.
- In area: 7-Eleven, near Shangri-La/JPark resorts, ~10-15 min from airport.

## Malls
- Mactan: Gaisano Grand Mall Mactan (local, groceries+cinema), Mactan Town Center (biggest),
  SM City Mactan, City Times Square.
- Cebu City (day trips): SM City Cebu, Ayala Center.

## Restaurants price bands (Mactan, for filtering)
- Jollibee / Mang Inasal (fast food) ₱300-500 ($5-9)
- Local carinderia / sutukil grill (fresh seafood) ₱400-700 ($7-13)
- Mactan Newtown restaurants (casual dining strip) ₱800-1,500 ($14-27)
- Waterfront / hotel restaurants (nicer, harbor views) ₱1,500-2,500 ($27-45)
- Cebu lechon house (must-eat, on a city day) ₱500-900 ($9-16)

## Groceries (the money saver)
- SM Mactan / Gaisano Grand supermarkets — a week of groceries for 2: ~₱1,500-2,500 ($27-45) if cooking.
- Carbon Market (Cebu City day) — cheap produce/fish/meat, bring cash.
- 7-Eleven / sari-sari — snacks, drinks, phone top-up.

## General Cebu/Mactan intel
- Sep = wet season — afternoon showers pass fast, mornings clear; pack light rain jacket.
- Tap water NOT safe — bottled everywhere. Grab over taxis. Cash is king outside big resorts.
- Must-eat: Cebu lechon, sutukil (grilled seafood), danggit (dried fish breakfast).
- "Po"/"Opo" to elders; don't touch anyone's head.
- Resort day passes exist if wanted: Savoy/Belmont ₱1,088-1,288, BE Resort ₱1,250, 1521 ₱1,200,
  Palmbeach ₱1,500, Shangri-La ₱2,000. (Island hopping 2-person private = ₱4,350-5,800/person —
  blows the budget; skip on a scouting trip.)
