# Philippines Cultural Intelligence

## Laws
- **Visa:** US citizens get 30 days visa-free
- **Drinking age:** 18
- **Drugs:** ZERO tolerance — life imprisonment possible
- **Smoking:** Banned in public places, restaurants, transport
- **Dress code:** Cover shoulders/knees at churches, government buildings, rural areas
- **Photography:** Ask permission before photographing locals, especially indigenous communities
- **Tipping:** Not mandatory but appreciated (10% at restaurants)
- **Emergency:** 911 (landline), 117 (police), 911 (mobile)

## Cultural Norms
- "Po" and "Opo" show respect to elders
- Don't point with feet, don't step on food or thresholds
- Don't touch someone's head (considered sacred)
- Don't embarrass or shame someone publicly ("hiya" = shame)
- "Filipino time" is real — expect flexibility
- Accept offered food/drink (hospitality)

## Safety
- Use Grab (ride-sharing), avoid random taxis
- Some Mindanao regions need caution (check advisories)
- Typhoon season June-November

## Food/Water
- Tap water NOT safe — stick to bottled
- Street food generally safe, watch hygiene
- Must-try: Adobo, sinigang, lumpia, halo-halo, lechon

## Metro Manila Specific
- Makati: Business district, walkable, Poblacion nightlife
- BGC: Modern, strict building rules, upscale
- Grab is cheapest transport (₱300-400 from airport)
- MRT for rush hour (fastest)
- Rush hours: 7-10am, 5-8pm (avoid if possible)
