# Circle 2026 Cohort 2 — Application Guide

## Program Overview
- **Program:** Circle 2026 Cohort 2
- **Focus:** Projects building on USDC, CCTP, Programmable Wallets, Smart Contract Platform, Arc
- **Format:** Web form with 7 sections
- **Key differentiator:** Circle wants USDC everywhere. Frame your project as expanding USDC utility.

## Application Sections

### 1. Applicant Details
Standard personal/company info. Solo founders are fine — no team required.

### 2. Project Abstract
- **Project Name:** Keep it descriptive (e.g., "GenTech Labs — Agentic Treasury")
- **One-line description:** One sentence only. "An autonomous multi-chain treasury protocol for AI agents."
- **Problem/Solution:** Lead with what's already shipped, not what you'll build
- **Barriers:** Be honest about what made this hard (regulatory, technical, adoption)
- **Why you:** Solo builder who ships. Production infrastructure, not whitepaper.

### 3. Project Alignment Track
- **Live in production?** Yes/No — be honest
- **Live on Arc?** Arc mainnet not yet live — "No (testnet ready)" is acceptable
- **Circle products integrated:** Check what you actually use (USDC, CCTP, etc.)
- **Circle products planned:** Check what you'll integrate with grant support

### 4. Milestones
- Add 3-4 milestones with target dates
- Each milestone should be a concrete deliverable (deploy, integrate, launch)
- Dates should be realistic — Q1/Q2 2027 for development work

### 5. Project Timeline and Roadmap
- **Funding status:** Self-funded is fine — shows independence
- **Where funds go:** Specific line items (contract dev, audits, ecosystem dev)
- **Technical roadmap:** Phase-based with Circle product integration milestones
- **Grant impact:** How the grant accelerates vs starts the work

### 6. Deck and Demo
- **Video demo:** Loom or YouTube walkthrough of working product
- **Investor deck:** Google Slides or PDF, 10 slides max
- **Links:** GitHub, website, social media, previous grants

### 7. Conflict of Interest
- Select "No" unless you have a relationship with a Circle employee

## Positioning for Circle
- **USDC is the hook** — your x402 gateway settles in USDC
- **CCTP is the expansion** — cross-chain USDC for multi-agent treasuries
- **Arc is the home base** — single-currency treasury ops
- **Programmable Wallets** — agent-native key management
- **Narrative:** "Circle wants USDC everywhere. Agents need to pay everywhere."
