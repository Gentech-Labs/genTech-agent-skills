## Why agent-focused programs, not generic AI/fintech

Generic accelerators (Pear VC, Technovation, EDA ups kill, Databricks, UMass) are
weak signal for us. Agent-native / crypto-AI programs are higher-signal: they
fund the exact x402 + ERC-8004 + agent-economy lane we build in. When Jordan
shares a random accelerator post, scan for the agent/crypto angle FIRST and only
then decide whether it's worth an application.

## Alliance DAO (alliance.xyz) — ALL19, verified live Aug 11

- **Deal:** $500K at $5M post (SAFE + 1:1 token side letter — standard, no token launch required)
- **Duration:** 12 weeks — 2 wk in-person NYC (wk 1-2) → 8 remote (wk 3-9) → 1 wk NYC Demo Day (wk 10)
- **Cohort start:** ALL19 starts Jan 11, 2027. **Review is YEAR-ROUND** — apply anytime,
  interview decision within 1 week, and Alliance funds *before* the cohort if your
  fundraise timeline is tighter. Do NOT wait for the cohort date to apply.
- **Regular app deadline:** Sep 23, 2026. Apply early — early apps get more review attention.
- **Median outcome:** $3.5M raised at $25M post after Demo Day (Paradigm, Multicoin, Dragonfly network)
- **Selectivity:** ~1,700+ apps, ~20 accepted/cohort (~1%). Founder-first — funds builders who ship, not decks.
- **Eligibility:** technical founders, pre-product accepted ("pre-product, pre-revenue, mid-pivot, anything
  goes. The only thing we care about: you want to build something big and your team can ship"),
  crypto/AI/fintech, solo founders OK. Most Alliance startups never launch a token. Visa/O-1 support available.
- **Alumni:** Pump, Pendle, Synthetix, Tensor, Caldera, Zerion, Primodium, 0x
- **In-person reality:** ~3 weeks total NYC (2 start + 1 Demo Day). Fully free otherwise.
  If Alliance funds us, this is the "quit Amazon + fly to NYC" trigger — Jordan confirmed he's
  fine with the NYC commitment at that funding level.
- **Existing draft:** `09-Green Room/alliance-application-draft.md` (Jul 17) — VERIFY it reflects
  ALL19/Jan-11 before submitting; it may cite older ALL18 cohort dates.

## Subspace Foundation Grant (Autonomys Network) — queued

- **Program:** subspace.foundation/grants-program — milestone-based grants for AI3.0 / decentralized AI
- **Instrument:** milestone-based grants in gas credits, $AI3 tokens, stablecoins, or USD (up to 10% of AI3 token supply)
- **Eligibility:** individuals, teams, nonprofits worldwide — **no residency, fully remote, no in-person. Easy lift.**
- **Grant types:** Infrastructure · AI-Powered dApp · Research · Community · Integration.
  **Integration Grant** (bridges/plugins/SDKs) and **AI-Powered dApp** (explicitly includes "on-chain
  agents capable of dynamic decision-making") are natural fits for x402 rails.
- **Apply:** docs.google form on the grants page. Process: prepare → apply → discovery → funding → showcase.
- **Status (Aug 11):** NOT yet applied — queued.
