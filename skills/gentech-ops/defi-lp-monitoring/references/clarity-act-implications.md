## What It Is

The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (CLARITY Act) ends the SEC vs CFTC turf war over digital assets. CFTC gets authority over "digital commodities" (most tokens), SEC is limited to securities-like assets. House passed 294-134 (Jul 2025), Senate Banking advanced 15-9 (May 2026). Final text approved by Trump Jul 16, 2026. Vote expected before August recess.

## Why It Matters for LP Strategy

**Structural, not cyclical.** This is not a rate cut or a halving. It removes the regulatory risk premium that has been priced into every token since the SEC enforcement campaign began. Compliance departments can finally build frameworks. Custodians can expand. Fund managers can allocate.

**The global regulatory race:**

| Country | Timeline | What |
|---------|----------|------|
| US | Aug 2026 (deadline) | CLARITY Act — market structure |
| Russia | Sep 1, 2026 | Licensed crypto market (5 categories) |
| India | Drafting | DELTA Act — real estate tokenization |
| Japan | Already has it | Expanding framework |
| EU | MiCA live | Full regulatory framework |

If the US stalls, capital flows to Tokyo, London, Dubai. Institutions are already plugged in — they just need compliance sign-off.
