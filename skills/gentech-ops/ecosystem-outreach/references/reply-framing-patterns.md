## Pattern 1: Remote Work Priority (Highest Urgency)

**When:** You need income NOW to leave full-time job. User is time-sensitive.

**Structure:**
1. Acknowledge their framing
2. State priority: remote work first, grants second
3. Show proof of ship (recent deployments)
4. Ask what they can offer (reciprocal)
5. Offer call/next step

**Template:**
```
That framing makes sense. Here's what would help most:

1. Remote builder opportunities (top priority) — I'm at Amazon full-time (12hr shifts Thu-Fri). Remote work that pays well is how I escape Amazon and build Gentech full-time. If you know teams needing someone with agent infra, x402 payments, or Avalanche experience, I'm ready to start tomorrow.

2. Grant support — Avalanche ecosystem grants are huge. If you can intro me to grant programs or help navigate applications, that accelerates things. We've got 48 live x402 endpoints and agent identity deployed — should be an easy sell.

3. Teams integrating our endpoints — Great for adoption and reputation, but doesn't pay the bills immediately. If you have leads, I'll follow up, but remote work/grants take priority.

Quick proof we ship:
- Today: Deployed 3 revenue APIs (Agent Registration, DeFi Intelligence, Agent Search)
- Live: 48 x402 endpoints via Bazaar
- Active: Avalanche ERC-8004 agent identity on C-Chain

What's your timeline? If you have remote leads now, let's connect them. If grant support is faster, let's start there.

Happy to hop on a call to discuss either.
```

**Why it works:**
- Anchors conversation on user's actual need (Amazon exit)
- Shows urgency without desperation
- Proves capability (deployed today)
- Reciprocal ("what's your timeline?")
- Clear next step (call)

## Pattern 2: Grant Application (Ecosystem Contribution)

**When:** Applying to ecosystem grant programs or accelerator programs.

**Structure:**
1. What you're building (1 sentence)
2. What you've already shipped (proof of execution)
3. What the grant will unlock (specific impact)
4. Community benefit (how it helps ecosystem)
5. Timeline + deliverables (concrete plan)

**Template:**
```
GenTech is building the infrastructure layer for AI agents — identity (ERC-8004), payment rails (x402), and data intelligence (DeFi, search).

What we've shipped:
- 48 live x402 endpoints via Bazaar marketplace
- Agent Registration API (ERC-8004) on Avalanche C-Chain
- DeFi Intelligence API (live today, monitoring LP positions)

What this grant unlocks:
- Deployment of 3 more revenue APIs ($33k/yr potential)
- Cloudflare x402 Gateway integration (production monetization)
- Open-source Agent Kit v2 (enables 100+ agent builders)

Community benefit:
- More agents = more transaction volume for Avalanche
- x402 payments = on-chain revenue for protocols
- Agent Kit = lowers barrier to entry for builders

Timeline: 8 weeks, weekly deliverables on GitHub.

Happy to discuss technical details or provide demo.
```

**Why it works:**
- Focus on ecosystem benefit (not just personal need)
- Concrete metrics (48 endpoints, $33k/yr)
- Clear timeline (8 weeks)
- Demonstrates ship velocity

## Pattern 3: Protocol Partnership (Value Exchange)

**When:** Reaching out to protocol teams for partnership/integration.

**Structure:**
1. Compliment their work (specific detail)
2. Connect your tech to their problem
3. Propose concrete partnership (not vague)
4. Show you can deliver (recent ship)
5. Offer demo or POC

**Template:**
```
Saw your new yield aggregation product — solid work on the curve optimizer.

We're building DeFi Intelligence API that tracks LP positions across protocols. Your aggregator could use our data to auto-rotate positions based on live APY/volatility.

Partnership opportunity:
- We integrate your protocol into our monitoring
- You get real-time LP efficiency analytics for your users
- Shared x402 payment endpoint for your API (we handle billing)

We ship fast — deployed 3 APIs today, 48 live endpoints on Bazaar.

Happy to build a POC this week if you're interested.
```

**Why it works:**
- Personal compliment (shows you know their work)
- Specific connection (your tech solves their problem)
- Clear value exchange (both sides benefit)
- Proof of capability (ship fast)

## Common Pitfalls

**Pitfall 1: Vague compensation target**
- **Problem:** "I'm flexible on comp" — wastes time, you'll get lowball offers
- **Fix:** State the number: "$5-6k/mo to leave Amazon"
- **Why:** Shows you're serious, sets floor for negotiations

**Pitfall 2: Pitching VCs instead of ecosystem**
- **Problem:** Focus on TAM, growth, traction — wrong audience
- **Fix:** Focus on shipping, grants, dev adoption — ecosystem cares about builders
- **Why:** Avalanche Builder Group wants builders who ship, not hypothetical startups

**Pitfall 3: Not showing proof of ship**
- **Problem:** "We're building X" — could be vaporware
- **Fix:** "Deployed X today" — proves you ship
- **Why:** Trust is everything. Proof > Plans.

**Pitfall 4: Only taking, not giving**
- **Problem:** Just asking for help/recs/intros
- **Fix:** "What can you offer?" or "What do you need?"
- **Why:** Reciprocity builds relationships. One-sided asks die.

**Pitfall 5: No clear next step**
- **Problem:** Conversation dies after initial reply
- **Fix:** "Hop on a call?" or "What's your timeline?" — drives action
- **Why:** Momentum kills ambiguity. Without next step, nothing happens.

**Pitfall 6: Generic messaging**
- **Problem:** "I'm interested in opportunities"
- **Fix:** "Remote work (agent infra/x402) or grants (Avalanche ecosystem)"
- **Why:** Specificity gets specific responses. Generic gets ignored.

**Pitfall 7: Not stating availability clearly**
- **Problem:** "I can start soon"
- **Fix:** "25hr/week immediately" or "full-time after leaving Amazon"
- **Why:** Ambiguity wastes everyone's time. Be precise.

## Ecosystem-Specific Notes

### Avalanche Builder Group
- **Cares about:** Builders who ship, real tech, ecosystem contribution
- **Doesn't care about:** VC-style pitches, TAM projections, hypothetical traction
- **Best angle:** "Working tech deployed today + grant opportunity"
- **Wrong angle:** "We're going to be the Stripe for agents" (too VC, too vague)

### Protocol Teams
- **Cares about:** Integration that works, solves their problem, can ship fast
- **Doesn't care about:** Your long-term vision, team structure, funding needs
- **Best angle:** "We integrate with your protocol + we can ship in 1 week"
- **Wrong angle:** "We're building a platform" (too vague, too slow)

### Foundation/Grant Programs
- **Cares about:** Ecosystem benefit, clear timeline, measurable impact
- **Doesn't care about:** Your personal situation, unrelated goals
- **Best angle:** "What we unlock with this grant + how it helps Avalanche builders"
- **Wrong angle:** "I need this grant to leave Amazon" (personal need, not ecosystem benefit)

## Conversation Flow

**Initial Contact:**
1. They reach out (DM, X comment, Telegram)
2. You reply with clear priorities + proof of ship
3. They ask for more details
4. You provide builder profile + concrete asks
5. They offer leads/intros
6. You follow up immediately

**Follow-Up (3-5 days later):**
- "What's the timeline on those remote leads?"
- "Any update on grant program intros?"
- "Happy to provide more technical details"

**Maintaining Momentum:**
- Don't wait for them — check in every 3-5 days
- Offer updates: "Just shipped X, wanted to share"
- Keep value flowing: "Saw this, thought of you"

**When They Respond:**
- Thank them immediately
- Move to next step (call, application, intros)
- Provide what they asked for (builder profile, demo)
- Don't leave conversation in limbo

## Reply Checklist

Before sending outreach:
- [ ] Clear priorities stated (remote work first, grants second)
- [ ] Proof of ship included (recent deployments)
- [ ] Builder profile ready (comp target + live links)
- [ ] Ask back included ("What can you offer?")
- [ ] Next step offered ("Hop on a call?" or "What's timeline?")

After sending:
- [ ] Follow up in 3-5 days if no response
- [ ] Ship something new (proof of continued progress)
- [ ] Offer technical details if interested
- [ ] Track leads in vault (HQ/approvals.md or separate outreach tracker)
