# Language Protocol — Per-Recipient Rule (Jordan, Aug 17, 2026)

## The Rule

**Speak ENGLISH to Jordan, always.** Use a collaborator's native language ONLY when replying to that collaborator directly.

| Recipient | Language |
|-----------|----------|
| **Jordan** | English, always |
| **Non-technical students** | their native language, warm + encouraging |
| **Vanito** | English (or whatever he uses) |
| **Dadrian** | English |

## Why

Jordan does not understand Cebuano/Tagalog. If you reply to him in a student's language, he cannot read it. The rule is **per-recipient, not per-topic** — a message *about* a student still goes to Jordan in English.

## The Incident (Aug 17, 2026)

After verifying a student's profile, Gentech replied to Jordan in Cebuano ("done ang double check"). Jordan corrected:

> "speak in English whenever you're speaking and replying to me. Whenever you speak with a non-English speaker, that's when you can speak in Tagalog."

**Root cause:** Gentech was in "student context" (verifying her profile) and carried that language into a reply to Jordan. The language must be chosen by WHO you're replying to, not the subject matter you were just working on.

## How to Apply

1. Before replying, identify the recipient (Jordan vs Vanito vs Dadrian vs guests).
2. Choose the language by recipient, not by topic.
3. If you were just working on a non-English speaker's context, reset to English before replying to Jordan.
