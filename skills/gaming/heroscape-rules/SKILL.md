# Heroscape Rules & Army Building

> Class-level skill for answering Vanito's Heroscape: Age of Annihilation / Rising Tide rules
> and army-building questions, including resolving disputes with other players. Vanito plays
> the Skordyre/insect faction (Queen Maladrix + Oathbound + Festering Honor Guard).

## When to Use
- Vanito asks about Heroscape unit points, legality, abilities, bonding, or army construction.
- A fellow player disputes the legality of Vanito's army and he needs a grounded rebuttal.
- Any rules-lawyer argument that hinges on "too many X" / "too many turns" / "you can't attack
  while bonding."

## Verify Before Answering
Always confirm point costs and card text against the official sources (do NOT guess):
- **Point costs:** `heroscape.fandom.com/wiki/<Unit>` (Stats section lists Points)
- **Card text / abilities:** `heroscapers.com` "Book of <Unit>" threads (exact printed text)
- **Rules:** Renegade Game Studios official Master Game rulebook PDF; heroscapers.com rulebook
- When the user names an unfamiliar unit, confirm it actually exists and its exact name before
  reasoning about it (e.g. "Skydive Infantry" is really **Skordyre Infantry**).

## Core Rules Facts (verified)
- **Unique vs Common:** a Unique unit may be drafted only ONE copy; a Common unit may be drafted
  as many as the point cap allows. There is **NO limit on how many DIFFERENT unique squads** you
  can field — only one copy of each named unique card.
- **Order Markers cap turns:** every player gets exactly 3 numbered Order Markers (1,2,3) plus X
  per round. No power, pheromone, or bonding adds extra turns — it only lets ONE revealed marker
  activate extra cards. That is legal and is the identity of bonding armies.
- **"Take a turn with" a unit = FULL turn (move AND attack).** Bonding/pheromone-activated units
  attack normally unless the card explicitly says "move without attacking." This is how ALL bonding
  works in Heroscape (Knights + Sir Gilbert, Romans + Marcus, etc.). A bonding army is legal,
  points-paid, and not an exploit.
- **Army size (figure count) has no rules weight.** Equal points can mean very different figure
  counts (wide cheap swarm vs few expensive heroes). Both are legal; size is a tactical tradeoff,
  not a violation.

## Resolving Rules-Lawyer Disputes (Vanito's scenario, Aug 2026)
Vanito's opponent repeatedly claimed his Skordyre swarm army was illegal. Every claim was wrong:
1. "Too many unique squads" — WRONG. Only one copy of each unique card is restricted; multiple
   different uniques are legal.
2. "Army looks too big / more figures than mine" — WRONG. Equal points = different figure counts.
3. "Too many turns in one round" — WRONG. Turn count is hard-capped at 3 order markers; bonding
   doesn't add turns, it sequences activations within one.
4. "Pheromone Coordination lets you attack while bonding" — CORRECT and legal. The card says
   "take turns with" which includes attack. Quote the card.
The clean rebuttal: "It's printed on the card. Take a turn includes attacking. Same as every
bonding army. The points paid for it." Stay calm, quote card text, let dice settle it.

## Pheromone Coordination (Skordyre Infantry, Thormun's Thunder) — exact card text
> "Before taking a turn with Skordyre Infantry, you may take turns with up to 2 Skordyre or Insect
> Common Heroes you control with different names, or 1 Skordyre or Insect Unique Hero you control."

- Bounded: triggers only on a Skordyre Infantry marker; coordinates at most 2 commons (different
  names) or 1 unique per trigger. Chaining MORE than the card allows (e.g. coordinating Maladrix
  AND Phalanx AND Legionnaires off one marker) WOULD be illegal — keep it within card text.
- Each coordinated hero takes a FULL turn (move + attack).
