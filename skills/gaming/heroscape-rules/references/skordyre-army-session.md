# Skordyre Army Dispute — Session Detail (Aug 2026)

Vanito's opponent repeatedly claimed his Skordyre swarm army was illegal. Researched against
official sources. Every claim was wrong; the army is legal.

## Source URLs used
- **Points / stats:** `heroscape.fandom.com/wiki/Queen_Maladrix_the_Conqueror` (160),
  `.../Oathbound_Phalanx` (80), `.../Oathbound_Legionnaires` (50), `.../Festering_Honor_Guard` (60)
- **Card text:** `heroscapers.com/threads/the-book-of-skordyre-infantry.65449/` (Pheromone
  Coordination exact text); the various "Book of ..." threads for each Skordyre unit.
- **Rulebook:** Renegade Game Studios Master Game rulebook PDF; heroscapers.com bonding glossary.

## The four claims and verdicts
1. "Too many unique squads" — WRONG. Unique restricts only ONE copy per named card, not the count
   of different unique squads.
2. "Army looks too big / more figures" — WRONG. Equal points = different figure counts; size is a
   tactical tradeoff, not a violation.
3. "Too many turns in one round" — WRONG. Turn count hard-capped at 3 numbered order markers; the
   X marker and bonding do not add turns, they sequence activations within one.
4. "Pheromone Coordination lets you attack while bonding" — CORRECT and legal. "Take turns with"
   = full turn incl. attack, identical to all bonding (Knights+Gilbert, Romans+Marcus).

## Pheromone Coordination exact text (Skordyre Infantry)
"Before taking a turn with Skordyre Infantry, you may take turns with up to 2 Skordyre or Insect
Common Heroes you control with different names, or 1 Skordyre or Insect Unique Hero you control."

Boundary to watch: coordinating MORE than the card allows off one marker (e.g. Maladrix AND
Phalanx AND Legionnaires) would be illegal. Keep within card text.

## Rebuttal wording that worked
"It's printed on the card. Take a turn includes attacking. Same as every bonding army. The points
paid for it." — stay calm, quote card text, let dice settle it.
