# Strategic Pivot Documentation & Social Generation

## The "Desmond Content Engine" Workflow

When a major strategic pivot or new product layer is discussed (e.g., shifting from "Bug Bounties" to "Life Optimization Platform"), the content engine automatically documents the change and generates social posts.

### Trigger
- User discusses a new vision/mission in HQ.
- New products are added to the stack (e.g., Gaming AI, Language Learning).
- A "Desmond Content Engine" cron job runs every 48h.

### Action Steps
1. **Scan Vault Changes**:
   - `09-Green Room/` for new ideas (`Autonomous-Language-Learning.md`, etc.).
   - `01-Agency/` for mission/manifesto updates.
2. **Identify the Narrative Arc**:
   - *Before:* What we used to be (e.g., "Bug bounty hunters").
   - *After:* What we are becoming (e.g., "Building the Amazon Prime of AI").
   - *The Hook:* "We want you to win in life."
3. **Generate Drafts**:
   - **X/Twitter:** Punchy, visionary, 1-2 sentences. Focus on the shift.
   - **Facebook/LinkedIn:** Story-driven. "Used to find cracks in code. Now building the ecosystem."
4. **Delivery**:
   - Post drafts to the **Entertainment** group for Jordan's review.

### Content Pillars for GenTech Pivots
- **The Journey:** From auditing others' code to building our own platform.
- **The Stack:** The "Prime" model ($15/mo for everything: Trust, Gaming, Travel, Language).
- **The Philosophy:** "You only lose if you're not paying attention."
- **The Tech:** AI companions that know your build, your life, and your goals.

### Example Post (Bug Bounties -> Building)
> Used to be about finding bugs in other people's code. Now? We're building the ecosystem.
> GenTech isn't just a security tool. It's a platform for winning.
> Gaming. Trading. Learning. AI companions that actually help you level up.
> We're all winners here. 🚀
