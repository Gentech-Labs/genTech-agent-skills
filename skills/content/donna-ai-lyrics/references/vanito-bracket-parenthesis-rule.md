# Vanito's Own Words: Brackets vs Parentheses (July 11, 2026)

Direct quote from Vanito on how Suno AI / Donna AI treat brackets and parentheses:

---

## Parentheses ( )
These are treated as performance / delivery cues.
They suggest how a line is said, not that it's a literal lyric.

Examples:
- (spoken)
- (whispered)
- (half-sung)
- (ad-libs)

Suno often interprets parentheses as tone, style, or vocal behavior, and sometimes it won't sing the words at all — it'll speak them, echo them, or treat them like background flavor.

## Brackets [ ]
These are treated as structural or role markers.
They tell Suno what part of the song this is or who is performing it.

Examples:
- [Verse 1]
- [Chorus]
- [Bridge]
- [Male Vocal]
- [Female Vocal]

Suno relies on brackets to understand song structure, section changes, and voice switching. Brackets are "instructions," not lyrics.

## Big practical difference
- Put lyrics outside both.
- Use [brackets] to control structure and voices.
- Use (parentheses) sparingly to shape delivery — overuse can cause Suno to half-ignore lines or glitch them.

## Rule of thumb (Vanito's mental model)
- Brackets = architecture
- Parentheses = acting

Donna App works with this too heavily.
