## What This Example Demonstrates

1. **Voice tags** — `[MALE]`, `[FEMALE]`, `[DUET]` assign who sings each section
2. **Max two tags per header** — `[MALE][VERSE]`, `[MALE][SCREAM]` — never three
3. **Standalone `[MALE][SCREAM]`** — scream gets its own section, NOT stacked on `[BRIDGE]`
4. **Tone cues** — first line under each section header uses parentheses for delivery direction
5. **Line-level cues** — `(Male)` / `(Female)` / `(Both)` inside `[DUET]` for shared choruses
6. **Dialogue-to-Scream arc** — bridge dialogue (quiet KAGE → whisper HIKARI → cracked answer) leads into the standalone `[SCREAM]` explosion
7. **Mixed EN/JP** — light sprinkle style, mostly English with Japanese phrases at emotional peaks
