# Vanito's Donna AI Format — Approved Song Example

This file shows the EXACT format Vanito approved for HIKARI songs with Donna AI vocal generation.

## Structure Rules

1. Song title + artist/description header
2. `FEMALE` on its own line (all vocals are female unless marked otherwise)
3. Section headers use `[SECTION — emotional tone description]` format
4. Bracketed tone cues like `[scream]`, `[yell]`, `[growl]` go DIRECTLY next to lyrics
5. NO stars/dividers (`---` or `***`) between sections
6. Each section has a detailed emotional tone description
7. KAGE duet rule: enters for ONE verse, hits a [scream], then exits

## Approved Example

```
Sakura no Chikai (桜の誓い) — Cherry Blossom Promise
HIKARI Solo · Melodic J-Rock Ballad

FEMALE

[INTRO — soft, gentle hum, like a lullaby]
Mm-mm, mm-mm...

[VERSE 1 — quiet, fragile, like a distant childhood memory with a lump in the throat]
I remember spring, the cherry trees in bloom
You held my hand and chased away the gloom
The petals fell like snow around your chair
I didn't know that year would be your last one there

[PRE-CHORUS — voice starting to tremble, fighting to hold it together, slowly rising]
You looked at me with tired eyes, but smiled just the same
"Don't you cry for me," you said
"The flowers will bloom again"

[CHORUS — letting go, full chest voice, tears falling but strength shining through]
Sakura no chikai — under falling petals I swore
I would carry your light through every closing door
Sakura no chikai — even when the winter's long
I will bloom again, I will be strong
This is my promise, this is my song

[FINAL CHORUS — scream, all out, no holding back]
SAKURA NO CHIKAI! [yell] I will never let you down!
I'm wearing your strength like a second crown!
SAKURA NO CHIKAI! [growl] Through the fire and the rain!
Every time I fall, I'll rise again!
[yell] This is my promise!
[yell] This is my fight!
Carrying your light into the endless night!

[OUTRO — exhausted, gentle, like the storm passed, a sad smile]
Mm-mm, mm-mm...
The petals fall, but I stand tall
Mm-mm... I remember spring...
```

```
I left our town with nothing but a bag and broken heart
The city lights were cold but I knew where to start
Every stage I stepped on, I could feel you in the air
Your voice in every melody, your warmth in every prayer

When I feel like giving up, when the night won't end
I close my eyes and see your face
[scream] AND RISE UP ONCE AGAIN!

[KAGE fades out — steps back, lets her take it home]
```

## Approved Bracket Tags for Donna AI

- `[scream]` — full scream delivery
- `[yell]` — shouted/yelled delivery
- `[growl]` — guttural growl delivery
- `[aggressive vocal]` — general aggressive tone
- `[heavy rock vocals]` — rock/metal delivery
- `[aggressive yell]` — alternative to [yell]
