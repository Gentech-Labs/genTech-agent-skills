# Vanito — Canonical Song References

These are fully-approved Vanito songs that serve as reference for the correct format.
Use these as templates for new songs — match the vocal direction style, duet labeling, and bare Japanese text.

```
**[Chorus — Clean/screaming mix, soaring]**
Can you hear me now?
I'm clawing through the silence, gonna break this vow
The chains are getting heavy but I won't let go
Mō ichido — just one more blow

Blood moon rising through the night
I see the spark, my soul takes fire
Blood moon burning — I won't go down
Koko de mate — until the final round
```

**Key format lessons from BLOOD MOON:**
- Section headers: `**[Section — Vocal tone]**` with tone in the header
- Japanese is bare text — no asterisks
- All-caps for punchlines
- Dashes between English and Japanese: `Kono yami no naka de — there's no turnin' back`

## RISING SUN — J-Rock Duet (Screaming Male + Melodic Female)

```
**[Verse 1 — Male, growl to scream]**
[MALE - spoken]
Woke up to the sound of a brand new day
Fire in my chest, ain't no other way
Every step's a promise I made
Asu e no michi — not afraid

[MALE - rising to scream]
Shadows tryna pull me down
King of this town
Eyes on the horizon, burning bright
**MAE WO MUITE** — READY TO FIGHT

**[Pre-Chorus — Female, melodic]**
[FEMALE]
Kaze ga yobu — the wind's calling me
I can feel it in my blood, this is destiny
Toben da — spread my wings and fly
Underneath the endless sky

**[Chorus — Both, soaring]**
[BOTH]
**RISING SUN** — I'M ALIVE
Yume wo tsukame — TOUCH THE SKY
**RISING SUN** — NEVER DIE
Kono sekai de — WE FLY HIGH

**[Bridge — Both, harmony]**
[MALE - clean]
Donna toki mo — no matter what they say

[FEMALE - soft]
Mae e susume — keep moving every day

[BOTH]
The sun is rising, can you feel the heat?
This is my moment, I won't accept defeat

**[Final Chorus — Both, full power]**
[MALE - screamed] **RISING SUN** — I'M ALIVE
[FEMALE - belt] Yume wo tsukame — TOUCH THE SKY
[MALE - screamed] **RISING SUN** — NEVER DIE
[FEMALE - belt] Kono sekai de — WE FLY HIGH
```

**Key format lessons from RISING SUN:**
- EVERY line labeled with `[MALE]`, `[FEMALE]`, or `[BOTH]` on its own line
- Section headers still have tone, but per-line labels override when speaker changes mid-section
- Male parts: spoken/growl → screaming → screamed
- Female parts: melodic → soft → belt
- Bridge: male line → female line → both swell
- Final chorus: call-and-response per line

## KIZUNA (絆) — Bonds — HIKARI Solo (Melodic J-Rock Battle Anthem)

HIKARI's character anthem. About the bonds that keep her fighting.

```
**[Verse 1 — Medium energy, building confidence]**
I grew up where the ocean meets the shore
My father's hands knew work I never knew before
He used to say, "When the night gets dark my girl
Don't you forget — you're made of more than this world"

The waves don't stop
And neither will I
Every tear I've cried
Built the fire inside

**[Chorus — POWERFUL FEMALE VOCAL, soaring anthem]**
Kizuna wa tsuyoi — bonds are strong
Even when everything I love is gone
I carry them with me, they light the way
Through the darkest night, into the day
Kizuna — I'm never alone

**[Verse 2 — Stronger, more determined]**
I left my town with nothing but a dream to hold
The city lights were blinding but the streets were cold
Every stage I stepped on felt like standing in the rain
But I sang until my voice became the sun again

**[Bridge — Emotional, vulnerable, slow build]**
My father's voice still echoes in the wind
"Keep going, girl — this isn't where it ends"

**[Final Chorus — Half-time, maximum power, vocal belt]**
Kizuna wa tsuyoi — bonds are strong
Everything I've lost taught me to carry on
I wear them like armor, they light the way
Through the longest night, into the day
Kizuna — I'm never alone
```

**HIKARI solo song characteristics:**
- Themes: ocean/coastal hometown, father's memory, bonds, resilience
- Vocal energy flow: medium build → powerful chorus → determined verse 2 → emotional bridge → max power final chorus
- Japanese title with English meaning in parentheses: KIZUNA (絆) — Bonds
- Chorus uses the character's motto ("Kizuna wa tsuyoi" from HIKARI's bio)
- No male vocals needed (HIKARI is melodic female J-Rock)
- Battle anthem style: driving rhythm, empowering lyrics, soaring chorus

## HIKARI NO YOUNI (光のように) — Like Light — HIKARI Solo (Emotional J-Rock)

About being the light for someone lost in darkness (linked to KAGE's story).

```
**[Verse 1 — Soft, intimate female vocal]**
I found you in the shadows, didn't know your name
You had this fire burning but you hid it in the pain
They said we didn't fit — a scream and a melody
But opposites collide and make the world I want to see

**[Pre-Chorus — Melodic, rising intensity]**
You taught me that the broken parts still sing
I taught you that the silence isn't everything

**[Chorus — POWERFUL FEMALE VOCAL]**
I'll be the light if you can't see the dawn
I'll be the warmth when everything feels wrong
Hikari no youni — watch me shine
Even shadows need a light sometimes

**[Verse 2 — Stronger, more emotional]**
You carry worlds I'll never know behind your eyes
A boy who learned to scream because he couldn't cry
But standing next to you I feel the weight divide
Two halves of one whole walking side by side

**[Bridge — Emotional climax, swelling]**
They said we'd never make it
Two storms in different skies
But look at us now
Burning brighter than the sunrise
```

**HIKARI emotional ballad characteristics:**
- Themes: being a light for someone, two opposites completing each other, protecting
- Vulnerability in verses, power in chorus
- The song title is her own name ("Hikari" = light) — self-referential title
- Bridges often have defiant "they said we'd never make it" energy
- Works as either solo or duet (in duet form, KAGE would sing Verse 1, HIKARI Verse 2)

- Chorus hooks are 4-5 lines with a strong repeated phrase
- All HIKARI songs connect back to her backstory: father's passing, coastal hometown, bonds
- Bridge sections are where the emotional climax hits — often quiet before the power
