# OpenArt.ai Approach — Vanito's Analysis (Jul 28, 2026)

Vanito studied an Instagram post from @openart_ai showing their workflow and described it in detail.

## What They Do

- **Character sheets for EVERYTHING** — the room, the people, the main character, the background, the level layout, objects, color codes. Every single element has its own reference sheet.
- **Full storyboard** — scene by scene, image by image, just like we do
- **First animation triggers the next** — whatever happens in the first image of the video triggers the next image, keeping it flowing without breaking consistency

## What We Can Learn

### What We Did Right
- The guitar evolving with Sakura flowers over time was a happy accident that worked beautifully
- The strumming animation was an amazing touch
- The overall emotional arc is strong

### What Drifted (Consistency Issues to Fix in Review Pass)
- **Shoes** — present at the beginning, disappeared later, she's barefooted
- **Copyright poster** — still visible in early scenes where it should have been removed
- **Framed photo** — keeps changing between scenes
- **Petal stuck to guitar** — when she gripped it tightly, the petal stayed there instead of falling off naturally
- **Guitar design** — changed over time (though this was a happy accident that worked)

### Vanito's Conclusion
> "Next time we definitely make sure we character-sheet every single thing that we're coming up with so it stays consistent in the video. I think that's the biggest problem we're having."

## The Fix Strategy (Vanito-Approved)
1. **Finish the full film first** — don't stop mid-production to fix individual issues
2. **One complete review pass** after all clips are done
3. Fix everything at once in a batch
4. **Next time:** character sheet everything before generating a single keyframe
