# MUFFIN — Character Reference

## Species
Domestic Longhair — Tortoiseshell & White (Calico mix)

## Appearance
- **Coat**: Long, dense, fluffy. Mottled tortoiseshell pattern — black, dark brown, and orange/ginger patches blended together
- **Face**: Mostly black with a distinct orange/ginger stripe running down the center of the nose ("blaze" or "smudge")
- **Eyes**: Large, pale green-yellow (chartreuse)
- **Whiskers**: Very long white whiskers, prominent against dark fur
- **Chest**: Prominent fluffy white bib (ruff) — like a tuxedo
- **Paws**: All four paws have white "mittens" or "socks"
- **Tail**: Extremely long and bushy — like a plume or bottlebrush. Dark grey/black
- **Ears**: Small with tufted fur

## Signature Poses
1. **Sitting upright** — looking directly at viewer, white bib prominent
2. **Sploot** — lying flat on stomach, front paws extended, back legs splayed out
3. **At window** — standing on sill, looking out, tail up
4. **Batting at petals** — one paw raised, playfully swatting at floating cherry blossom petals

## Personality (from photos)
- Curious — always looking at something
- Relaxed — comfortable splooting on floors
- Alert — watches the world from windows
- Affectionate — looks up at her human
- Playful — bats at floating objects
