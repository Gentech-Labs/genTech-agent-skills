# Steve Harvey Product Announcement Script Structure

**Verified Jul 16, 2026** — Jordan corrected the framing from abstract (agent economy) to concrete (specific app + benefit). This reference captures the preferred structure for ALL product announcement scripts targeting non-technical audiences.

## Core Principle: Concrete Before Abstract

Never open with the technology. Open with the user's experience, the problem being solved, or the enabling event — then reveal what GenTech built.

## The 5-Part Structure

```
1. HOOK — The enabling thing that happened (1 sentence)
2. RESPONSE — What GenTech built because of it (1 sentence)
3. BENEFIT — What the app actually does (2-3 sentences)
4. AUDIENCE — Who it's for (1-2 sentences)
5. CLOSE — Punchy, memorable, future-facing (1 sentence)
```

## Worked Example (GenTech Food — Jul 16, 2026)

**Hook:** "They just gave AI agents the ability to DoorDash. You heard me. Agents can order food now. They can handle the whole delivery."

**Response:** "So what do we do at GenTech? We built an app around it. It's called GenTech Food."

**Benefit:** "You open the app and you tell it what you're craving. Soul food. Filipino. Thai. Jamaican. Whatever culture you're from, whatever you grew up eating. GenTech Food finds it based on where you live. It pulls up recipes, it finds restaurants near you that serve it, and if that spot is close enough, you tap once and order."

**Audience:** "We made this for the people who miss the food from back home. For the people who want to discover something new without the guesswork. Your favorite dishes. Your culture. Right where you are."

**Close:** "The agent economy is not a buzzword. It is delivering food to your door. GenTech Food. Coming soon."

## Why This Works

- **Non-technical audiences immediately understand the value** — they don't need to know about x402, ERC-8004, or agent-to-agent protocols
- **The technology becomes the answer to a question the audience already has** — "agents can DoorDash? cool, what did you build?"
- **Each part builds on the last** — hook → response → benefit → audience → close creates a natural narrative arc
- **Steve Harvey's delivery** of this structure feels like a friend explaining something exciting, not a press release

## Anti-Pattern (What Jordan Rejected)

❌ Start with the abstract: "The agent economy is building and it ain't slowing down. Every single day there are builders grinding."

✅ Start with the concrete: "They just gave AI agents the ability to DoorDash. So we built GenTech Food."

The abstract approach sounds like a LinkedIn thought leader. The concrete approach sounds like news. Always pick news.
