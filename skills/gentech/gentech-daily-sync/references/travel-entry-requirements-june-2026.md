# Travel Entry Requirements Research - June 2026

## South Korea (Incheon Airport Transit)

### Current Status for Americans
- **K-ETA Exemption**: Active through 2026 (temporary)
- **Transit < 24 hours**: No visa required
- **Leave airport**: Optional K-ETA recommended ($9)

### Requirements Summary
- **Document**: Valid US passport
- **Flight**: Must have connecting ticket (no immigration stay)
- **Duration**: Transit time < 24 hours
- **Cost**: Optional K-ETA $9 if leaving airport

### Recommendations
- **For 4+ hour layover**: Apply for K-ETA to potentially explore Incheon
- **Airport only**: Fine without K-ETA during exemption period
- **Official source**: k-eta.go.kr for application

## Philippines (Clark International Airport)

### Entry for Americans
- **Visa-Free**: Up to 30 days tourist stay
- **Requirements**: Valid US passport + return/forward ticket proof
- **Extension**: Possible at local immigration office
- **Purpose**: Tourism (4-day visit fits perfectly)

### Additional Documents to Carry
- Passport (minimum 6 months validity recommended)
- Return flight confirmation
- Accommodation details
- Emergency contact information

### Clark → Manila Transport
- **Bus**: Primary option (2-3 hours)
- **Cost**: PHP 200-400 (~$3-6)
- **Companies**: Several bus operators available
- **Schedule**: Frequent departures

### Local Transportation
- **Clark Airport**: Various transport to city center
- **Angeles City**: Tricycles, jeepneys, taxis
- **Manila**: Bus terminals near major transport hubs

### Safety & Practical Tips
- **Currency**: Have PHP for local transport/food
- **Connectivity**: Consider local SIM/eSIM
- **Weather**: August is rainy season - umbrella needed
- **Time zone**: UTC+8 (same as Manila)
