## ⚠️ Four KAGE Looks — Evolving Design

Vanito uses **multiple KAGE appearances**. Always check which version is current before generating any character image. **When in doubt, ask Vanito to send his current reference image — never generate from text description alone.**

- **Status:** OLDER DESIGN — may have been superseded

- **Source:** kage-poster-original.jpg

### Look 3: Current KAGE (Confirmed 2026-07-17 — Blood on the Strings)
This is KAGE's current design, confirmed via Vanito's own reference image:
- **Hair:** Jet black, medium-length, messy, WET/CLUMPY look — NOT spiky, NOT dry — falls forward partially over face and eyes. Chaotic, grunge visual kei style.
- **Face:** Head thrown back, eyes closed or rolled back, mouth open screaming/agonized expression. Pale skin with blood splatter. Sharp angular jaw visible in profile.
- **Clothing:** Plain BLACK t-shirt ONLY — no jacket, no vest, no denim. Short sleeves. No front graphics/text.
- **Necklace:** Silver chain with RECTANGULAR pendant (dog tag or bar style) — NOT a star or cross.
- **Tattoos:** FULL BLACKWORK SLEEVES on BOTH arms — dense dark tribal/geometric patterns extending from shoulders to hands. Hands also heavily inked/dark.
- **Guitar:** Black FENDER STRATOCASTER-style electric guitar (double cutaway, three pickups). White handwritten text on body: "KAGE" (upper bout), "NOT DONE YET" / "DONE YET" (lower bout). Black strap with Japanese text: "血が出るまで".
- **Blood:** Heavy blood on both hands, smeared on guitar strings and fretboard, splattered on face and t-shirt.
- **No:** Jacket, studs, skull tee, star pendant, cross earring, waist chain, or bat wing embroidery in this look.
- **Use for:** Blood on the Strings cover and current KAGE depictions
- **Source:** Vanito's reference image (hub at music/vanito/blood-on-the-strings-reference.jpg)
- **Style note:** Grungier and less ornate than Look 1 — stripped down, tattooed, more raw/visceral.

### Look 4: Artist Tab / Iconic KAGE (Confirmed 2026-07-17 — Band Identity)
This is KAGE's "band icon" design from the artist tab on Vanito's hub. Used for the KAGEKŌ identity/portrait rather than song-specific covers.
- **Pose:** Back turned to viewer, standing tall facing the horizon/moon. Face NEVER visible.
- **Hair:** Medium-short, spiky messy black, covers ears and nape of neck. Not wet, not chin-length.
- **Coat:** Long black distressed leather trench coat/duster with heavily tattered shredded hem (hanging ribbons). High popped collar.
- **Back Emblem:** Large crimson red design on the back of the coat — stylized winged cross or phoenix with spread wings. Looks painted in blood.
- **Chain:** Thick silver chain hanging from waist/left hip.
- **Pants:** Tight black pants/jeans.
- **Boots:** Heavy black combat boots.
- **Guitar:** Black Gibson Les Paul Custom style (single cutaway, humbuckers, silver hardware). Silver chain wrapped loosely around the body and neck.
- **No:** T-shirts visible, pendant, tattoos, or blood on hands/face (from behind).
- **Setting:** Tokyo rooftop at night. Blood Moon in the sky. Tokyo Tower illuminated red on the left. Black crows/ravens flying. Dark storm clouds. Red and black color palette.
- **Use for:** Band identity, artist tab portrait, full-album icon
- **Source:** hub at music/vanito/kage-yami-no-naka-de.png
- **Style note:** Gothic visual kei — the most ornate and iconic look. Not grunge/raw like Look 3. This is the "poster/merch" KAGE.
- **Role:** Guitarist (does NOT sing)
- **Age:** 22 | **Height:** 178cm | **Blood Type:** A
- **Hair:** Messy spiky black, chin-length sides, falls diagonally left-to-right covering one eye (left)
- **Face:** Sharp angular jaw, high cheekbones, pointed chin, thick bushy eyebrows angled up at outer corners, sharp intense dark eyes, narrow nose bridge, thin dark lips, pale skin
- **Jacket:** Distressed black leather biker jacket, RED LINING on collar lapels, silver pyramid studs on collar and shoulders, large bat/demon wing embroidery between shoulder blades
- **Shirt:** Black graphic tee with white skull-with-antlers woodcut-style print
- **Pendant:** Multi-pointed star or cross (confirm with user — he has both versions)
- **Earring:** Cross-shaped dangle on LEFT ear
- **Waist:** Silver chain with padlock hanging from left hip
- **Jeans:** Black, tight, ripped at knees and thighs
- **Boots:** Heavy black lace-up combat boots
- **Rings:** Thick silver bands on ring fingers (both hands)
- **Guitar:** Black electric — Dean ML or Flying V style (Look 1 default). 
  **Song-specific variant:** For Yami no Naka De / Kimi no Koe era art, the confirmed guitar is a **gloss black Les Paul double-cutaway** with red blood-like splatters and drips on the lower body, two humbucker pickups (black/dark covers), Tune-o-matic bridge with stopbar tailpiece, four reflector-style control knobs, pickup selector toggle on upper bout, and silver chain strap. Always reference the specific song's established art before choosing which guitar to render.
- **Personality:** Silent/calm, carries something from the past, dedicates everything to music, clumsy with human relationships

## Art Style
Dark gothic visual kei digital painting. NOT pure anime (too cartoonish). NOT photorealistic (too real). The style of the character sheets — detailed painted concept art with dramatic lighting and textured brushwork. Heavy reds, blacks, deep crimsons, with warm moon glow as contrast.
